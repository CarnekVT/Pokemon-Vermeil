VERMEIL Visual 2.5D
Version: 5.10.1
Milestone: Surface Geometry Consistency Hotfix
Build: 2026-08-13

Key changes:
- Mountain top surfaces, generated cliff faces, collision, camera and support elevation read the same SurfaceGeometry grid.
- Stair cells no longer duplicate a flat MountainTop surface underneath the ramp.
- Explicit level 0 overrides remain authoritative even on legacy MountainTop tiles.
- Generated volume/cliff geometry uses the central height grid; MountainWall remains art/material only.
- Explicit height cells elevate ordinary P0 tile art too; Terrain Tags are no longer required just to lift a surface.
